New Heading

Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

Summary: New Heading does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal information. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Information you enter

The app asks for your retirement or Fleet Reserve date and whether you are an officer or enlisted. You can also mark items complete, write notes, and add your own tasks. All of this is saved only in the app's storage on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Information we collect

None. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. We do not receive your name, email address, location, contacts, or device identifiers.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders, the app asks for permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your device using your retirement date. No information is sent to a server to deliver them. You can turn reminders off in the app's Settings or in your phone's settings.

Links to other websites

The app links to official government websites (for example, MyNavy HR, VA.gov, TRICARE, and DFAS). Those links open in your browser, and those sites have their own privacy policies.

Deleting your data

Use Settings > Erase all data in the app, or delete the app. Because we do not have your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

New Heading is intended for adult service members and is not directed to children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
