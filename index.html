Common questions

Where is my data stored?
Only on your phone. There is no account and nothing is sent to us. See the privacy policy.

How do I move my progress to a new phone?
Progress is stored on the device only, so it does not transfer. Enter your retirement date on the new phone and check off completed items.

Who is this for?
U.S. Navy officers and enlisted members retiring or transferring to the Fleet Reserve. Separation without retirement is not covered yet.

Is this official guidance?
No. New Heading is a planning aid. Confirm every item with your Fleet & Family Support Center, personnel office, or the linked official source.
